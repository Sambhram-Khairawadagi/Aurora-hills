## 1. Deep Market Analysis: Hubballi & Dharwad Buyer Psychology

To achieve **100% high-converting qualified leads** and avoid wasting clicks, we must understand the distinct mindsets of buyers in Hubballi and Dharwad. Although they form a twin municipal corporation (HDMC), their cultural, financial, and buying psychologies are completely different.

### A. The Hubballi (Hubli) Buyer Persona: The Commercial Wealth Builder
* **Who they are**: APMC traders (cotton, chili, food grains), logistics operators, industrialists in Gokul Road & Tarihal, private hospital doctors, wholesale merchants in Durgadbail, civil contractors, and business families.
* **Financial profile**: High liquid cash reserves, strong disposable income, and looking for pride of ownership and capital appreciation.
* **Their pain point in Hubli**:
  * Central Hubli (Keshwapur, Vidyanagar, Shirur Park) is extremely congested, polluted, and expensive (₹6,000–₹12,000/sq.ft).
  * Finding a peaceful, green plot within a true gated community inside Hubli is nearly impossible.
* **Why they buy at The Aurora Hills in Dharwad**:
  * Dharwad is considered the **"cooler, greener, elite hill retreat"** (often referred to locally as "Chhota Mahabaleshwar").
  * Just 15–20 minutes drive along the 6-lane NH-4 corridor.
  * Ideal for building a prestigious weekend villa, a retirement home, or a high-return land asset.
* **Winning Triggers for Hubli Buyers**:
  1. **Lifestyle Prestige**: Grand clubhouse, resort-style infinity pool, championship tennis & pickleball courts.
  2. **Festive Gold/Silver Incentives**: 50 Grams Gold on 40×60 plots and up to 1.5 KG Silver on 30×50/30×40 plots touches deep North Karnataka cultural values of "Bhoomi & Bangara" (Land & Gold).
  3. **High Appreciation**: Located directly along the high-growth Dharwad NH-4 bypass corridor.

---

### B. The Dharwad Buyer Persona: The Cautious, Legal-First Intellectual
* **Who they are**: Karnatak University professors, SDM Medical/Dental College doctors and staff, High Court bench advocates, senior government/KPTCL officials, IIT/IIIT Dharwad faculty, and retired professionals.
* **Financial profile**: Salary & pension savings, fixed deposits, seeking secure, zero-risk generational wealth.
* **Their primary fear & anxiety**:
  * North Karnataka has seen numerous unauthorized "Gram Panchayat" or litigated layouts lacking layout sanctions, water pipelines, or clear title deeds.
* **Why they buy at The Aurora Hills**:
  * **HDUDA & NA-KJP Approved**: 100% legal government clearance.
  * **Pre-approved Bank Loans**: Backed by SBI, HDFC, ICICI (up to 80% financing). In Dharwad, an SBI approval is considered the ultimate stamp of legal purity.
  * **Within Dharwad City Limits**: Near prestigious schools (Podar, SDM) and universities (KUD).
  * **Infrastructure in Place**: 1.5 Lakh Litre overhead water reservoir, underground electrical cables, and 30ft/40ft wide asphalt roads.
* **Winning Triggers for Dharwad Buyers**:
  1. **Legal Safety First**: "HDUDA Approved", "Clear Title", "SBI Loan Approved".
  2. **Tranquility & Microclimate**: Sunset Viewpoint, Sadhankeri/Kelgeri lake breeze, zero pollution.
  3. **Generational Asset**: A place where their children and grandchildren can live peacefully.

---

### C. The Metro Expat / NRI Persona (Bengaluru / Pune / Abroad)
* **Who they are**: Techies, corporate executives, and NRI professionals who grew up in North Karnataka and now want an ancestral footprint or a secure retirement villa plot in Dharwad.

## 3. Campaign Settings & Configuration

When creating the campaign in Google Ads, use these exact parameters:

* **Campaign Type**: `Search Network Only`
  * ⚠️ *Critical Rule*: **Uncheck** "Include Google Search Partners" and **Uncheck** "Include Google Display Network". Display Network burns local real-estate budgets on irrelevant mobile apps.
* **Campaign Goal**: `Leads`
* **Campaign Name**: `Search - The Aurora Hills - Hubli Dharwad Plots`
* **Bidding Strategy**:
  * **Phase 1 (First 14 days)**: **Maximize Clicks** with a **Maximum CPC Bid Limit of ₹32.00**.
    *(This prevents Google's automated algorithm from bidding ₹80+ on day one while it learns).*
  * **Phase 2 (After 30 conversions)**: Switch to **Maximize Conversions** with a Target CPA of ₹300.
* **Location Targeting**:
  * Select: **"Enter another location"**
  * Target:
    * `Dharwad, Karnataka, India` (City)
    * `Hubli, Karnataka, India` (City)
    * Radius: `30 km radius around Kelgeri, Dharwad`
    * Optional High-Intent Metro Campaign (Separate ₹200 budget or selective keyword): `Bengaluru` (only for phrase match `"plots in dharwad"`).
  * **Location Options (Crucial)**:
    * Target: Select **"Presence: People in or regularly in your targeted locations"** *(DO NOT select "Presence or interest" as it shows ads to random users outside Karnataka)*.
* **Languages**: `English`, `Kannada`
* **Ad Schedule**:
  * `Monday – Sunday: 07:30 AM – 10:00 PM`
  * *(Ensures your sales team can immediately call back or WhatsApp prospects within 5 minutes of form submission).*

## 9. 3-Minute Quick Setup in Your Open Google Ads Window

Since your Google Ads dashboard (`social.propertybasket@gmail.com`) is already open on your screen:

1. **Step 1: Start New Campaign**
   * On the left navigation bar, click the blue **`+` (Create)** button -> Click **Campaign**.
2. **Step 2: Objective & Type**
   * Objective: Select **Leads**.
   * Campaign Type: Select **Search**.
   * Conversion Goal: Select **Submit lead forms** and **Phone call leads**.
3. **Step 3: Campaign Settings**
   * Name: `Search - The Aurora Hills - Hubli Dharwad Plots`
   * Networks: **Uncheck** Display Network; **Uncheck** Search Partners.
4. **Step 4: Location & Languages**
   * Select **"Enter another location"** -> Type: `Dharwad` and `Hubli`.
   * Location Options: Choose **"Presence: People in or regularly in your targeted locations"**.
   * Languages: Select **English** and **Kannada**.
5. **Step 5: Daily Budget & Bidding**
   * Budget: Enter **₹1,200.00**.
   * Bidding: Select **Clicks** -> Check **"Set a maximum cost per click bid limit"** -> Enter **₹32.00**.
6. **Step 6: Keywords & Ads**
   * Paste the keywords from Section 4 into the keyword box.
   * Paste the 15 Headlines and 4 Descriptions from Section 6 into the Ad creation box.
   * Add Sitelinks and Call Asset (`+91 8970198701`).
7. **Step 7: Negative Keywords**
   * Go to **Keywords** -> **Negative Keywords** -> Click **`+`** -> Paste the list from Section 5.
8. **Step 8: Review & Publish**
   * Review all settings and click **Publish Campaign**.
